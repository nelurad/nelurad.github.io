Research Focus

I study how the human mind uses shared underlying structure to connect our seemingly distinct systems, such as perception, language processing, memory, and decision-making. My goal is to investigate these common cognitive rules by aligning human behavior with our computational models. I work with Dr. Mike Kaschak in the Learning, Memory, and Language Lab.
