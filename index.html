Research Focus

I study how the human mind uses shared underlying structure to connect our seemingly distinct systems, such as perception, language processing, memory, and decision-making. My goal is to better understand the principles that organize cognition, drawing on both human behavior and computational approaches. I work in the Learning, Memory, and Language Lab with Dr. Kaschak.
